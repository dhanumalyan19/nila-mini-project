# Nila

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Municipal assessment officers combine building records, regional surveys and measurements. A later public locality view must not expose private records.

## Product Purpose

Support qualified decisions, explain evidence and missing information, and fairly plan assessments under limited capacity. Never diagnose health conditions or certify safety.

## Operating Context

User-specified local Windows development, Flask, MySQL, server-rendered HTML/CSS, vanilla JavaScript and Leaflet. Work proceeds only one user-approved phase at a time.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Regional radiation and indoor radon are separate tracks. Units, source, time, quality, workflow and synthetic provenance remain explicit. Unknown is not zero. Imports must preview row errors before saving. Maps must have a list fallback. Priority changes require explanation; import is not scientific review.

## Evidence on Hand

The user's original specification, current implementation and explicitly synthetic offshore demonstration data. No real local survey data or officially approved screening thresholds have been supplied.

## Product Principles

- Preserve scientific and privacy boundaries.
- Explain decisions and uncertainty.
- Keep shortfalls visible; selection does not resolve unmet need.
- Preserve user records and audit changes.
