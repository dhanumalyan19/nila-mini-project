# Phase 5 officer workspace extension

Mode: Operate. User approved validated imports, geographic associations, switchable map layers and list fallback. This is a functional extension of the established interface, not a redesign. The user's instruction to make ordinary in-phase decisions independently governs; no extra design approval cycle or generated mockup is needed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make the distinction between imported evidence and an assessment decision visible at every save and map view.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve the existing light paper surface, dark blue-green text, teal controls, compact system typography, thin table rules and clear focus outlines.

STORY: Officers choose a file, examine numbered valid/error rows, confirm with a reason, and inspect saved evidence by track. Map layers support the evidence list, never replace it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Standard navigation and page heading; imports present a narrow upload form and sample links. The map presents track/provenance filters, labeled layer toggles and a wide geographic view, followed by a server-rendered evidence list.

FORM: Direct, precisely specified extension of existing forms/tables. No concept-seed applies. Primary actions are preview and explicit commit. Map movement respects reduced-motion preferences.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
