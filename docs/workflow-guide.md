# Phase 7 — measurement review and follow-up guide

Nila records operational decisions for qualified officers. It does not diagnose a
health condition, set a scientific threshold, prescribe mitigation, or certify
that a building is safe.

## Officer workflow

1. Open **Review & actions** and enter a complete measurement record, selecting
   an allowed track/type/unit combination. The record is saved as submitted and
   the matching assessment case becomes **awaiting review**.
2. Record a quality decision with a rationale. An accepted record moves the
   case to **action required**, meaning an officer must decide what comes next;
   no action is created automatically. A rejected or information-needed record
   returns the case to evidence collection.
3. If an action is appropriate, choose an action-required case, optionally
   cite matching track/provenance guidance, assign a responsible officer and
   record the decision reason. The case becomes **action assigned**.
4. A reported completion is only a report. It moves the case to **action
   reported complete** and requires separate follow-up measurement evidence
   plus a reviewer rationale.
5. The follow-up reviewer records whether more evidence, further action, or an
   outcome review is appropriate. Only the final reviewer choice can move a
   case to **outcome reviewed**.

Guidance entries retain their source, applicability and review status. They are
references—not automatic radiation or mitigation instructions. Every submitted
measurement, measurement review, action update and follow-up decision appears
in the private workflow audit history with actor, reason and state change.

## Important boundaries

- Indoor radon and regional radiation choices stay separate via the measurement
  catalogue and matching assessment case.
- Units, source, timestamps, instrument and quality are retained with each
  record. Unknown data is never turned into zero.
- Manual entry inherits the building's synthetic provenance; synthetic and
  real records cannot be silently mixed through a guidance or follow-up choice.
- CSV measurement preview/commit remains available under **Import data**. An
  import does not itself perform qualified measurement review.
